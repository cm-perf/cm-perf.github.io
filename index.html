03 / FAQ
What is a 2-step secondary rev limiter?

A launch-control rev limit on your M1/M2 buttons. Hold the brake and throttle to hold a set RPM and build boost, then launch. It sits below your main redline as a secondary limit.

Which BMW engines do you support?

N20, N26, N55, N63 and S55 are live now. B-series engines are in active development, advancing quickly. If your engine is not listed, ask us.

How is it delivered?

Worldwide through Bootmod3. Find us in the Bootmod3 tuner directory, or send an enquiry and we flash a map to your account. No shipping, no workshop visit.

What do I need for it?

A Bootmod3 licence for your car and the usual flash hardware. It works with factory or aftermarket M-button wheels, using stock pedals only.

What does it cost?

From $200 USD for a standard 2-step. Stage builds and bespoke ECU ROM work are quoted per car. Email contact@cmperf.uk.
